School and Hobbies

School subjects that I enjoy consist of:

  • Mathematics, because of its logic. I particularly enjoy algebra.
  • Science, specifically physics and chemistry so I can see the complex working of the world
  • History, because of the topics and the essay writing

I enjoy these subjects because they all have an element of structure in them. For math and science, they have formulae. In history, it consists mainly of book work and writing essays.

As for hobbies, I enjoy writing. I mainly write small skits using some of my original characters, and sometimes attempt writing chapter books with little success.
